# Influence Platform

**Live website:** https://influencer-frontend-kappa.vercel.app

Influence Platform is a website where **businesses find, hire and work with talent**: creators, journalists, speakers, experts and public figures. It keeps everything in one place, from the first search to a signed agreement and a final review.

This guide explains **every page, every tab and every button** in plain language. You do not need any technical knowledge to read it.

## 1. The idea in one minute

Finding the right person for a campaign, event or story is usually slow. Follower numbers are scattered across apps, you never know who is real, and deals happen over chats with nothing in writing.

Influence Platform fixes this in four steps:

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **1. Discover** | Search talent by profession, industry, topic, country, city, language and number of followers. |
| **2. Connect and hire** | A verified business sends a **hire request**, or posts a **campaign** that talent can apply to. |
| **3. Agree and sign** | Both sides agree on the work, milestones and payments in a digital **agreement**, then sign it with a code sent to their email. |
| **4. Deliver and review** | The talent delivers each milestone, the business approves it, and at the end both sides leave a **star review**. |

The platform is **free to use** right now. It is a platform, **not an agency**: businesses deal with talent (or their manager) directly. Payments are arranged between the two sides; the platform records what was agreed.

## 2. The four kinds of accounts

When you sign up you choose one of three account types. The fourth (Admin) is for the platform's own team.

| Account | Who it is for | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Talent** | Creators, journalists, speakers, experts, public figures | Claim or create a public profile, list services and prices, set availability, receive hire requests, apply to campaigns, sign agreements, get reviews, choose a manager. |
| **Business / Organization** | Companies, brands, marketing/PR agencies, NGOs, universities, event organizers, government bodies | Get verified, hire talent, post campaigns, keep shortlists, compare people, draft and sign agreements, leave reviews. |
| **Manager / Agent** | People or agencies who represent talent | Get verified, link with talent (only with the talent's approval), manage their profiles and services, answer their hire requests and handle their agreements. |
| **Admin** | The platform team | Approve profile claims, verify businesses and managers, handle reports and disputes, manage users, read the audit log and the newsletter list. |

> **"Verified"** means the platform team has checked that the person or company is real. Only verified businesses can hire, and only verified talent can be hired.

## 5. Creating an account, logging in and passwords

### Sign up
https://influencer-frontend-kappa.vercel.app/register

1. **Step 1, choose your account type:** **Talent**, **Manager / Agent** or **Business / Organization**. Each card explains who it is for.
2. **Step 2, fill the form for that type.** Fields marked * are required.
   - **Talent:** full name, email, password.
   - **Manager / Agent:** full name, agency / company name (leave empty if you work on your own), country, website or LinkedIn (optional), email, password.
   - **Business / Organization:** type of organization, business/organization name, country, website (optional), work email, password.
3. Press **Create account**. You land on your dashboard. You can go back with **Change account type**.

**Sign up with Google:** instead of an email and password, you can press **Sign up with Google** under *Login details*. Your name and email come from Google and no password is needed. Businesses and managers fill in their organization and country details first.

Passwords need at least 8 characters.

### Log in
https://influencer-frontend-kappa.vercel.app/login

Enter email and password and press **Log in**. You stay logged in on that device until you log out.

Or press **Sign in with Google**. If no account uses that Google email yet, you are sent to the sign-up page. If you already have an account with the same email, Google is linked to it automatically.

### Forgot password
https://influencer-frontend-kappa.vercel.app/forgot-password

Three short steps:
1. Enter your email and press **Send code**.
2. Check your inbox for a **6-digit code** (it expires in 10 minutes; check spam if needed) and type it in. You can **Resend code** after a short wait.
3. Choose a **new password** and press **Save and sign in**. Other devices are signed out for safety.

## 6. Talent: dashboard and every tab

Open **Dashboard** from the top bar. A row of tabs appears at the top (on phones it scrolls sideways), plus a **View public profile** link.

### Tab: Overview
- Greeting *"Hi, [name]"* with your email.

- **Claim progress** (if you claimed or created a profile): a 4-step tracker **Claim sent → Code sent to you → Code verified → Approved**. When the team sends you a code, a box appears here to **enter the 6-digit code**. After 5 wrong tries it locks and the admin reviews it.
- Notices such as *"New hire requests waiting for your reply"* or *"Managers asked to represent you"*.
- Shortcut cards: Services & rates, Availability, Hire requests, Agreements, Open campaigns.

### Tab: My profile
Edit everything shown on your public profile:
- **Profile photo** at the top (**Upload / Change photo**, **Remove**; JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 2 MB).
- **Identity:** headline, bio, country, city, languages. (To change your name, contact support.)
- **Social accounts:** at least **one is required**. For each: platform, profile link, handle, followers and engagement %. **+ Add account** to add more.
- **Website** (optional).
- **Categories:** professions, industries, topics.
- **Save changes**.

### Tab: Services & availability
- Three summary boxes: **Work status**, **Services** (*X live of Y*) and **Profile status**.
- **Your services** (up to a set maximum):
  - **Add service** opens a form: service name, type (Keynote / talk, Brand campaign, Sponsored post, Podcast guest, Event appearance, Workshop, Consulting, Other), what's included, **price** (Fixed price, Price range, or Ask for a quote), currency, priced per (project, event, post, hour, day), delivery time in days, and **Show on my profile** on/off.

  - With no services yet you can start from templates: Keynote talk, Brand campaign, Podcast guest.
- **Availability:**
  - **Open for work / Not taking work** switch.
  - **Open to:** Speaking, Brand campaigns, Podcasts, Events.
  - **Usually replies:** within 24 hours, 3 days, or a week.
  - **Available from** date and a **note for businesses**.
  - **Save availability** (the page warns you about unsaved changes).
- Tip: to show **"Available for hire"**, turn on *Open for work* **and** keep at least one service live.

### Tab: My applications
Every campaign you applied to, with the business, your pitch and the status: **Under review**, **Selected**, **Not selected** or **Withdrawn**, plus what that means. If selected: **Open hire request**. While under review: **Withdraw** (asks you to confirm). **Find campaigns** takes you to the campaigns page.

### Tab: Hire requests
Requests from verified businesses that want to hire you.
- **Waiting for your reply** at the top. Each shows the business (verified, rating, location, website), the project title, service, message, budget and start date.
- **Accept** or **Decline**, with an optional note. Accepting **shares your name and email** with the business and shows you theirs.
- **Earlier requests** below, with their status and *"Answered by [manager]"* if your manager replied.

### Tab: Agreements
Your agreements with businesses (see [Journey: an agreement](#115-an-agreement-from-draft-to-review)). Each line shows the title, status, the business, total amount, last update and **what to do next** (e.g. *Review the terms and sign*, *Deliver the next milestone*, *Leave a review*).

### Tab: My manager
- **Requests from managers** who want to represent you: **Accept as my manager** or **Decline**.
- **Your current manager**, since when, and **what they may do** for you (tick boxes):
  - **Manage profile:** edit your profile, services, prices and availability.
  - **Handle hire requests:** answer hire requests and handle your agreements (negotiate, sign on your behalf, deliver, review). Every signature shows it was made on your behalf.
- **Save permissions**, **Remove manager** (with confirmation).
- **Rate your manager** with stars and a short review (shown on their public page).
- **Invite your manager** by the email they log in with, with an optional message.
- **Past managers and requests**.

## 9. Admin panel: every tab

Admins see **Admin panel** in the top bar instead of Dashboard. Tabs are along the top (scroll sideways on phones), plus a **Manage profiles** link to add or edit profiles directly.

### Dashboard
*"What needs your attention today"*: claims needing action, open reports, users (and how many are suspended), profiles (claimed, verified, hidden), businesses and managers waiting for review, open disputes and active agreements, and users by role. Each box has an **Open** link.

### Claims
People saying *"this profile is me"*, or talent who created a **new profile**.
- Filters: **All open, Needs my action, All claims, New profiles**.
- **Claim detail page:** the profile, the claimant (name, email, join date), the evidence (official accounts, email, note) and, for new profiles, every submitted detail.
- **Code verification:** choose where to send a code, **Generate code**, copy it, send it to the talent as a message on that account, then **Done, I sent it**. You see when the code was sent, wrong attempts (out of 5) and when it was verified.
- If locked after 5 wrong tries: **Reset OTP and resend**, **Verify manually**, or **Reject**.
- **Approve claim** (only after the correct code) or **Reject** with a reason the user will see.
- **History** of every admin action on that claim.

### Businesses
Business verification. Search by company or website and filter by status. The detail page shows company details, the account, the same **code verification** flow as claims, and **Verify business**, **Reject** (with reason) or **Revoke verification**.

### Managers
Managers waiting for verification. Check their website/LinkedIn and proof links, then **Verify manager** or **Reject**. The page also lists the talents they are linked to. Revoking ends their links with all their talents.

### Agreements
Every agreement between businesses and talent: title, business, talent, status, last update. The detail page shows the signed version (with time and IP of each signature), milestones, deliveries and reviews. For a **disputed** agreement, the admin chooses a decision and writes a note to both sides:
- **Continue the work:** back to active, as signed.
- **Mark as completed:** the work counts as done; both can review.
- **Cancel the agreement:** it ends here.

### Users
Search by name or email, filter by role and status. Actions: **Suspend / Unsuspend** (suspended users are logged out and can't log in) and **change role**. Every action asks for confirmation.

### Reports
Reports and takedown requests about profiles. Filter by status (**Open, Reviewing, Resolved, Rejected**) and reason. On the detail page the admin sets a new status, writes an admin note, and can **take the profile down** (hide it) or **delete it permanently** (it won't be added again from public sources).

### Audit log
A read-only record of **every admin action**: time, admin, action and target. Filter by action, target type and date range. **Show changes** opens a box showing who did it, when, on what, a one-line summary, and a table of exactly **what changed (Before → After)**.

### Newsletter
Everyone who subscribed from the footer: email, language, date and status. Search, **Export CSV** to download the list, or **Delete** an email.

### Manage profiles
Admins can **create a new public profile** or **edit any profile**: all profile fields, social accounts, visibility (visible or hidden), verified on/off, and the **source** of the information (public website/news, research sheet, sent by the person, admin knowledge).

## 10. Pages every logged-in user has

### Notifications
https://influencer-frontend-kappa.vercel.app/notifications

Everything that happens to you: new hire requests, accepted or declined requests, campaign applications and results, agreements to sign, deliveries, approvals, disputes, reviews, verification updates and manager requests.

### Settings
https://influencer-frontend-kappa.vercel.app/settings

- **Account card** with your **profile photo or logo** (upload, change, remove) and role.
- **Appearance:** **Light**, **Dark** or **System** (follows your device).

## 11. Full journeys, step by step

### 11.1 Talent claims an existing profile
1. Sign up as **Talent**.
2. Search your name on **Talent**, open your profile, press **Is this you? Claim**.
3. Add your **official account links** (Instagram, X, YouTube, website…), optionally an official email and a note, and **Send claim**.
4. The admin sends a **6-digit code** as a message to one of those accounts (usually within 1–2 days).
5. Enter the code on your **Overview** tab.
6. The admin approves. The profile is now **yours**, shows the **verified badge**, and only you (or your manager) can edit it.

### 11.2 Talent creates a new profile
If you can't find yourself: **Create your profile** from the dashboard. Fill in your details and at least one official social account. If a similar profile already exists, you're asked **"Is one of these you?"** so you can claim it instead. Your new profile stays **hidden** until the code is verified and an admin approves it.

### 11.3 Business gets verified and hires
1. Sign up as **Business / Organization**.
2. **Business details** → fill everything → **Submit for verification**.
3. Enter the **6-digit code** the admin sends you.
4. After approval you are a **verified business**.
5. Find a verified talent → **Hire** → choose a service (or "something else"), project title, message, budget, currency and start date → **Send request**.
6. The talent (or their manager) **accepts** → you both see each other's contact details → **Draft agreement**.

### 11.4 Business runs a campaign
1. **Campaigns** tab → **New campaign** → fill the form → **Publish campaign**.
2. Talent see it on the public Campaigns page and **apply** with a pitch. You get a notification for each application.
3. **Manage applicants** → **Select** or **Reject**. A selected talent gets an accepted hire request automatically, and you go straight to the agreement.
4. The campaign closes by itself when enough talents are selected; you can also **Close** or **Reopen** it.

### 11.5 An agreement, from draft to review
1. **Draft:** the business writes the agreement title, **scope of work**, **milestones** (each with an amount and due date), currency, payment terms, content usage rights and cancellation terms, then **Send to talent**.
2. **Agreeing terms:** either side can **Propose changes**. Each change creates a new **version**, and any signatures on the old version are removed. Earlier versions stay visible under *Earlier versions*.
3. **Signing:** each side presses **Email me a code to sign** and enters the 6-digit code. That is their electronic signature. When both have signed, the agreement becomes **Active**.
4. **Delivering:** the talent presses **Mark as delivered** on each milestone, with a note and an optional link.
5. **Approving:** the business **Approves** each delivered milestone. When all are approved, the agreement is **Completed**.
6. **Reviews:** both sides leave **1–5 stars** and a short review. These ratings appear on profiles and directory cards.
7. **Download PDF:** at any time, download a printable copy with the terms, signatures and a unique **fingerprint** that proves nothing changed after signing.
8. **If something goes wrong:** before both sides sign, either side can **Cancel agreement**. After it is active, either side can **Report a problem** (a dispute). Work pauses and an admin decides: continue, complete or cancel.

### 11.6 A manager starts representing a talent
1. Sign up as **Manager / Agent** → **Manager details** → **Send for verification** → the admin verifies you.
2. Open a talent's profile → **Request to represent** with a short introduction. (Or the talent invites you by email.)
3. The talent **accepts** and chooses your permissions (**Manage profile**, **Handle hire requests**).
4. The talent now shows *"Represented by [you]"*, and you work for them from **My talents**, **Hire requests** and **Agreements**.
5. The talent can change permissions or remove you at any time; either side can end it.

## 14. Trust, safety and privacy

- **Verification everywhere:** talent prove their profile with a one-time code; businesses and managers are checked by the team before they can hire or represent anyone.
- **Honest reviews:** reviews come only from **completed agreements**, so every star comes from real work.
- **Public data only:** unclaimed profiles are built from public sources, which are listed on the profile. Anyone can report a mistake or ask for removal.
- **Contacts are private** until a hire request is accepted.
- **Managers act only with the talent's permission**, and every action they take is shown as done on the talent's behalf.
- **Signed agreements** carry a fingerprint, signing time and record of who signed.
- **Admin actions are logged** in the audit log, and important actions always ask for confirmation.
- **Security:** limits on repeated login and code attempts (codes lock after 5 wrong tries), secure log-in sessions, and protected file uploads (images only, up to 2 MB).
- **Delete your account** any time from Settings.
